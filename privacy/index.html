Privacy

This site counts visits so I can see what people read and what to improve. It does that without cookies, without storing your IP address, and without following you to other sites. There are no ads and nothing is sold.

Visits: GoatCounter

Pages on joshuaheagle.com load GoatCounter, a small, open-source analytics service. For each visit it counts the page, the site you came from, your browser and operating system (not the full browser string), your screen width, your language, and your country (worked out from your IP address, which is not stored). GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser and doesn't share the data with anyone. It is run from Ireland and keeps the data on servers in Finland and Germany. GoatCounter's own privacy page has the details.

Cloakfall

The Cloakfall game also counts, anonymously, how it gets played: which game type people choose, how many players and robots, the hint setting, whether a game is finished or left early, and any errors the page runs into. These counts go to GoatCounter too. They never include a player's name, anything you type, or a room code.

Online games run through a small server in Canada (OVHcloud, Beauharnois, Quebec). It holds player names and network addresses only in memory, while you're connected, to run the game and limit abuse, and forgets them when you leave. It keeps no record of who connected.

Hosting

The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees the requests your browser makes; its privacy statement covers that.

Opting out

To stop your visits being counted in one browser, open any page with #toggle-goatcounter at the end of the address (for example joshuaheagle.com/#toggle-goatcounter) and confirm. Your browser remembers the choice; do it again to switch counting back on. Blocking gc.zgo.at with a content blocker works too, and the site works the same either way.
